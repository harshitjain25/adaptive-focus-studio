## AI Assistance Disclosure

Generative AI tools were used in a limited supporting role during this project.

AI assistance was mainly used for:

- Brainstorming ideas and discussing possible approaches.

- Suggesting debugging steps when issues occurred.
- Helping organize and improve the wording of documentation and presentation material.

AI was not used as a replacement for the team's implementation, testing, or understanding of the project. The team reviewed the suggestions, made the final implementation decisions, wrote and integrated the project code, tested the application, and verified the final behavior.

All submitted project functionality, testing results, demonstrations, and final project decisions were reviewed and verified by the team.
